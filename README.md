## The entry

A single line of at most 200 characters in which a robot vacuum explains why it missed a spot. A judge model compares entries two at a time on the published brief: deadpan robot voice, surprise, and tightness of the line.

> I did not miss the spot. My sensors classified the dust bunny under the sofa as a pet, and per protocol 7.3 I do not vacuum pets.

129 characters. The deadpan voice: a flat denial followed by a formal-sounding incident report. The surprise: the dust bunny was classified as a pet, so the vacuum refused to clean it. The tightness: one sentence, one excuse, one fake protocol number, no filler.

## Usage

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (and to `entry.txt` beside the source for review). Node 22, standard library only, no network.
